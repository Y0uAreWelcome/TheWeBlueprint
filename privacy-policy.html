Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information across the The Web Blueprint website, client portal, and the sites we build for you.

Last updated: July 31, 2026

1. Who we are

The Web Blueprint ("we," "our," or "us") designs, builds, and maintains websites for local businesses. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our marketing website, our client portal, and the process of building and hosting websites on behalf of our clients.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in a few different ways, depending on how you interact with us:

  • Information you give us directly — such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, and current website, when you submit our contact or mockup request form.
  • Client account information — such as billing details and site content, when you become a client and use the client portal.
  • Automatically collected information — such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through standard web analytics when you browse our site.
  • Payment information — processed securely by our third-party payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to mockup requests and inquiries
  • Design, build, host, and maintain your website
  • Process payments and manage billing for active clients
  • Send service-related communications, such as updates about your site or account
  • Improve our website and services

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. How we share information

We share information only where necessary to operate our business, including with:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that keep your website live, fast, and secure
  • Payment processors that handle billing and invoicing
  • Database and content-management providers used to power your site's editable content
  • Service providers who help us with email, scheduling, or customer support

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of The Web Blueprint, our clients, or others.

5. Data retention

We retain client information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, or record-keeping requirements. Mockup requests that don't convert to a client relationship are retained only as long as reasonably needed to follow up.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at theweblueprint.support@proton.me. If you're an active client, deleting certain information may affect our ability to continue hosting or maintaining your site.

7. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve the browsing experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — including encryption, access controls, and monitored infrastructure — to protect the information we and our hosting partners handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify active clients directly.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at theweblueprint.support@proton.me or (555) 010-2040, Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm.
